KSEB feasibility guide

Check transformer solar headroom.

Under KSERC regulations, rooftop solar injection is permitted up to 90% of your distribution transformer (DTR) rating. Follow the guide to check real-time transformer headroom on KSEB's official portal.

  1. Find your consumer number and electrical section on your KSEB bill.
  2. Open the reCap portal and enter them to see your transformer’s remaining solar capacity.
  3. No headroom? We file for augmentation, use zero-export, or arrange a feeder transfer.

Frequently asked questions

Got questions? We have answers.

Still unsure? Call +91 9061626868 and talk to an engineer directly.

What size solar system do I need for my home?
For most typical Kerala households consuming 300 to 450 units a month, a 3kW to 4kW on-grid solar plant delivers approximately 90% bill reduction while maximizing the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar central subsidy.
How much terrace space is required?
You need about 100 sq ft of shade-free roof for each kilowatt. A 3 kW system needs about 300 sq ft, and a 4 kW system about 400 sq ft. Elevated pergola structures can also preserve 100% of usable floor space.
How do I get the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy?
We submit the national portal documents, coordinate the KSEB inspection and follow up until the subsidy reaches your bank account. It usually arrives about 30 days after your bank details are accepted. Read the PM Surya Ghar subsidy guide.
What if my KSEB transformer has zero headroom?
We provide three tested engineering solutions: (1) Filing for DTR transformer capacity augmentation with KSEB, (2) Zero-Export limitation for 100% daytime self-consumption, or (3) Feeder transfer to an adjacent line with surplus headroom. See the KSEB feasibility guide.
How long does solar panel installation take in Kerala?
Physical installation of a 3kW to 5kW residential solar plant takes 1 to 2 days. The complete process including KSEB net-metering approval, feasibility inspection, and bi-directional meter installation usually takes 15 to 25 days.
How does rooftop solar generation work during monsoon weather in Kerala?
Bifacial Tier-1 solar panels generate electricity from direct, diffused, and reflected sunlight. While peak output is highest during clear days, modern solar panels continue generating 25% to 40% of their rated capacity even under overcast monsoon skies. How solar performs in the Kerala monsoon.
What maintenance is required for residential rooftop solar panels?
Rooftop solar systems have no moving parts. Periodic rainwater washing keeps panels clean in Kerala. Routine inspection of AC/DC protection boxes, lightning arresters, and chemical earthing pits every 6 months ensures optimal generation.
