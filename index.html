OFFICIAL KSEB GRID FEASIBILITY

Check Transformer Solar Headroom on the Official KSEB Portal

Under Kerala State Electricity Regulatory Commission (KSERC) regulations, rooftop solar injection is permitted up to 90% of your distribution transformer (DTR) rating. Follow the guide below to check real-time transformer headroom on KSEB's official portal.

Step-by-Step Instructions

How to Verify Feasibility & What to Do on Our Site

Before applying, ensure your local KSEB Distribution Transformer (DTR) has sufficient renewable energy headroom. Here is the exact process:

01

Find Your Consumer Number & Section on Your Bill

Keep a copy of your recent KSEB electricity bill ready. Note down your 13-digit Consumer Number (e.g., 1155482091234) and your Electrical Section name (e.g., 1155 - Cherthala South), printed on the top-left section of the bill.

02

Open the Official KSEB reCap Portal

Click the Open KSEB Feasibility Portal (reCap) button on the right to open Kerala State Electricity Board Ltd's official self-service capacity tracker (wss.kseb.in/selfservices/reCap).

03

Select Your Section & Distribution Transformer (DTR)

On the KSEB portal, select your Electrical Circle, Division, and Section Office (or input your Consumer Number / Transformer ID). This will display the list of all distribution transformers under your section.

04

Check the Available Renewable Energy Headroom

Review your transformer's Rated Capacity (kVA), Already Energized Solar (kW), and most importantly, the Balance Feasible Capacity (kW) available under the KSERC 90% injection cap.

05
What to Do Next on Our Site

Return to Sooryavamshi to Size & Register Your Project

Once you verify capacity on KSEB's portal, come right back to our site:

  • 🧮
    Calculate Exact System Size: Use our Solar Calculator to determine your ideal capacity (e.g. 3 kW – 10 kW), roof area requirement, and PM Surya Ghar central subsidy.
  • 📋
    Hand Over the Paperwork to Us: Fill out our Enquiry Form with your Consumer Number. Sooryavamshi's authorized engineers handle the formal KSEB Soura registration, onsite shadow audit, electrical inspection, and net-metering sync!

Dedicated Protection

Lightning & Surge Protection

Essential engineered defense: Rooftop solar systems naturally attract atmospheric electrical charge. We implement multi-stage suppression to safeguard your home.

  • Arrester: Early Streamer Emission (ESE) / Franklin Rod
  • Surge Devices: Class B+C / Type 1+2 SPDs
  • Grounding: 3-Pit Dedicated Chemical Earthing (< 5Ω)
Role in Household Electrical Safety:

A single direct strike or nearby utility grid surge can instantly destroy unprotected inverters, smart TVs, refrigerators, and home wiring. Sooryavamshi installs dedicated earthing pits for:

  • Pit 1: Lightning Arrester down-conductor
  • Pit 2: Inverter AC/DC electronics chassis
  • Pit 3: Solar PV module aluminum frame array
