Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

What size solar system do I need for my home? expand_more
For most typical Kerala households consuming 300 to 450 units a month, a 3kW to 4kW on-grid solar plant delivers approximately 90% bill reduction while maximizing the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar central subsidy.
How much terrace space is required? expand_more
You need roughly 80 to 90 sq.ft of shadow-free rooftop space per kilowatt. A standard 3 kW system requires ~260 sq.ft, while a 4 kW plant needs around 340 sq.ft. Elevated pergola structures can also preserve 100% of usable floor space.
How do I get the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy? expand_more
As an empannelled installer, Sooryavamshi submits all national portal documentation, coordinates KSEB physical inspection, and secures the Direct Bank Transfer (DBT) straight into your bank account within 30-45 days of commissioning.
What if my KSEB transformer has zero headroom? expand_more
We provide three tested engineering solutions: (1) Filing for DTR transformer capacity augmentation with KSEB, (2) Zero-Export limitation for 100% daytime self-consumption, or (3) Feeder transfer to an adjacent line with surplus headroom.
