Alappuzha district

Solar designed for coast and backwater homes.

We are based in Cherthala, in Alappuzha district, and install rooftop solar for homes from Alappuzha town down to Haripad, Kayamkulam, Mavelikkara and Chengannur.

Alappuzha roofs face salt air, humidity and long monsoon spells. In low-lying areas such as Kuttanad, we also plan cable routes and inverter placement above flood level. Every system gets corrosion-resistant mounting, chemical earthing and a lightning arrester.

Kerala's monsoon does lower solar output for a few months, but a well-sized system still cuts most of the year's bill. Our calculator uses Kerala sunlight figures, not national averages.

Questions we hear in Alappuzha

Frequently asked questions.

Still unsure? Call +91 9061626868 and talk to an engineer directly.

Do you install rooftop solar across Alappuzha district?
Yes. We are based in Cherthala and install across the district, including Alappuzha town, Ambalappuzha, Mararikulam, Kuttanad, Haripad, Kayamkulam, Mavelikkara and Chengannur.
Is solar worth it in Alappuzha with the long monsoon?
Yes. Panels still produce power on cloudy days, though less than in full sun. Over a full year a correctly sized system covers most of a typical home's KSEB bill, and the PM Surya Ghar subsidy shortens the payback.
Can you install solar in flood-prone areas like Kuttanad?
Yes. The panels sit on the roof, and we place the inverter, distribution boxes and meters above the known flood level so they stay safe when water rises.
How much subsidy can I get for rooftop solar?
Under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, homes get ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third kW, so a 3 kW or larger system gets the maximum ₹78,000. We file the application and the amount is paid straight into your bank account. Read the full subsidy guide.
How long does installation take?
Fitting a 3 kW to 5 kW home system takes 1 to 2 days on the roof. The whole process, including KSEB feasibility, inspection and fitting the net meter, usually takes 15 to 25 days.
