Analytics and cookies

How we see what works.

We use Microsoft Clarity to learn which pages help visitors and which confuse them, so we can fix them. Clarity only runs if you agree: by tapping Accept on the cookie banner at the bottom of the screen (it shows until you answer), or by switching it on below or in Settings. If you tap Reject, or don't answer, it doesn't load. Clarity makes recordings of visits that show taps, clicks, scrolling and mouse movements, and heatmaps (pictures of where people tap most). Clarity doesn't see what you type into our enquiry form. It does see the bill or units you enter in our calculator: on the screen, and in the web addresses of our buttons and of the next page you open.

Clarity keeps most recordings for 30 days, and heatmaps, the taps it records, a random sample of recordings and any recordings we save for up to 9 months. To recognise you when you come back, Clarity and Microsoft store cookies (small files) in your browser. They last from one day to about 13 months. We have told Clarity not to share data with Microsoft Advertising. Microsoft explains its own use of this data in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Cookie names, for the technically minded: _clck (1 year) and _clsk (1 day) from Clarity, and Microsoft cookies such as MUID (about 13 months).

Microsoft Clarity in this browser

This is your answer on our cookie banner. Change it here or in Settings (the gear button at the top of every page) at any time. Clarity runs only while this is on. Other browsers and devices keep their own setting.

Off

Tapping Reject or switching Clarity off also deletes its own cookies from this browser. Microsoft's cookies, such as MUID, are stored by Microsoft's websites, so we can't remove them. You can clear them in your browser settings.

Questions about Clarity? Ask our Grievance Officer.

Our site also remembers a few choices in your own browser, so they stay the same from page to page: light or dark mode, text size, animations, the sun cursor, and your answer on the cookie banner. These choices are kept in your browser, not in cookies, and we don't collect them. If Clarity is on, its recordings show the page as you saw it, including these choices. Apart from Clarity, this website sets no cookies.

Keeping and protecting it

How long, and how safely.

We keep your enquiry while we are helping you. If you become a customer, we keep your project details until your system's warranty ends or for as long as tax and other laws require, whichever is longer. If you don't go ahead, we delete your details once we no longer need them to follow up, or sooner if you ask. When we delete your details, we remove them from our database, our Google Sheet and our own records.

This website uses a secure (https) connection. Anyone can send us an enquiry, but no visitor can read enquiries back: only our team can, after signing in, and nobody else can create a login. If a data breach affects your details, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your rights

Your details, your say.

  • See them: ask for a summary of the details we hold about you and who we have shared them with.
  • Correct them: fix anything wrong, incomplete or out of date.
  • Delete them: we will delete your details unless the law requires us to keep them, for example tax records of a finished installation. Clarity's records are the exception: Microsoft deletes them on a fixed schedule (see below).
  • Change your mind: tell us at any time to stop using your details, as easily as you sent them. For Microsoft Clarity, just switch it off in Settings (the gear button at the top of every page) or under analytics and cookies; it stops at once. We stop from then on, but this doesn't undo what we already did with your agreement.
  • Complain: raise any concern with our Grievance Officer, below.
  • Name someone: choose a family member or another person who can use these rights for you if you pass away or are unable to act yourself.

To use any of these, WhatsApp or call us from the phone number you gave in your enquiry, or email us and tell us that number, so we can find your details and check it's you. It's free, and we reply within one month. Clarity doesn't store your name or phone number. To stop Clarity, use the switch: it stops at once, but it doesn't delete what Clarity has already recorded. Microsoft deletes most recordings after 30 days, and heatmaps, the taps it records, a random sample of recordings and any recordings we save after 9 months. Clarity can't delete one visitor's data without deleting everyone's, so we can't delete just yours sooner. If you have questions about it, ask our Grievance Officer.
