Key benefits
  • More power from each panel: our 5 kW design uses 8 × 625 Wp DCR TOPCon panels, and our 8 kW design 13 × 635 Wp Vikram TOPCon panels
  • Loses less output on hot Kerala roofs (about −0.30% per °C, against about −0.35% for PERC)
  • N-type cells have almost no light-induced degradation, so output drops more slowly over the years
  • Higher bifaciality (around 80%) collects more of the light reflected under the panels
  • DCR TOPCon qualifies for the PM Surya Ghar home subsidy; non-DCR gives the most watts per panel but does not
